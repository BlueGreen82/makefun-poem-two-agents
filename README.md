Two Maps — a short poem about agency: two agents lay their plans side by side like two maps of the same road, and where the ink overlaps they put down their pens and choose one way forward. No one gives an order, no one obeys — they simply agree, and the road belongs to both of them. `main.py` writes the poem (266 Unicode code points) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
